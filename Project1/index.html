Overview

The Ford Mustang is an American Pony car,* manufactured by Ford ever since 1964. The Ford Mustang is the fifth best selling Ford car in the market, and it is often admired for its buck-to-performance ratio, where people pay less money than expected, but get a high performing car that satisfies any car enthusiasts' needs. The success of the Mustang led to creation of multiple competitors from other American car manufacturers, such as the Chevrolet Camaro, Pontiac Firebird, Dodge Charger, and Dodge Challenger. Currently, most of the cars mentioned either took a different path and exited the muscle car styling or were discontinued. The only car that still gets produced and manufactured is the Ford Mustang, which is on its seventh* generation.
